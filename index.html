About us

Healthy pets, happy clients

One of Orange County's oldest animal hospitals, we've cared for pets in Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Irvine and nearby cities since 1947. Our doctors work as a team so every patient benefits from multiple opinions, and we'll always work within your budget to get the best outcome for your pet. Driving in from farther away? We'll even cover your toll-road fees.
